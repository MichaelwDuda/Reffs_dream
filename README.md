# A Reff’s Dream

> A comedic action roguelike where the referee is finally the hero.

---

## Overview

**A Reff’s Dream** is a fast-paced action roguelike where players take control of different sports referees and fight against endless waves of angry players and fans.

Each run allows players to discover new weapons, upgrades, and ridiculous combinations while surviving increasingly difficult enemies.

The game combines chaotic combat, humor, and replayability with sports-themed characters and abilities.

---

# Genre

- Action
- Roguelike
- Wave Survival
- Comedy

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

1. Fight waves of enemies
2. Earn weapons and upgrades
3. Improve your build during the run
4. Survive as long as possible
5. Start a new run with different builds and strategies

### Minor Gameplay Features
- Light platforming
- Character-specific movement mechanics
- Mid-run upgrades and item collection

---

# Story / Premise

After years of abuse from players, coaches, and fans, the referees finally snap.

Inside a strange dream world, every referee gains absurd powers based on their sport and must battle endless hordes of angry sports fanatics.

# Playable Characters

## Wheelchair Rugby Ref

### Starting Weapon
**Charge Chair**  
Rush forward and slam enemies with a wheelchair charge attack.

### Playstyle
- Aggressive
- Crowd control
- Burst mobility

---

## Soccer Ref

### Starting Weapon
**Yellow Card Toss**  
Throws yellow cards at enemies from range.

### Upgrade Path
Eventually upgrades into stronger **Red Cards**.

### Playstyle
- Ranged combat
- Precision attacks
- Status effects

---

## Frisbee Observer

### Starting Weapon
**Frisbee Throw**  
Throws bouncing frisbees that ricochet between enemies.

### Upgrade Path
Frisbees gain additional bounces and tracking abilities.

### Playstyle
- Trick shots
- Crowd clearing
- Combo focused

---

## Hockey Ref

### Starting Weapon
**Hockey Stick**  
Fast melee attacks with sweeping hits.

### Playstyle
- Fast movement
- Melee combat
- Hit-and-run gameplay

---

## Football Ref

### Starting Weapon
**Penalty Flag**  
Throws penalty flags that knock enemies backward.

### Playstyle
- Defensive
- Space control
- Knockback focused
